# Robot Learning POC

Use idle tasks for work planning and the active goal for the objective. The 50 cm ape-shaped robot is the target; earlier 35 cm work is historical evidence. Microduck, Microban, and Berkeley are references. Use upstream Arena, Isaac Lab, Mimic, LeRobot, and GR00T code where relevant. Work locally in this folder; Runpod is compute only.

`robot/ape-current-design.json` names the selected mechanical design candidate. Use its exact source and evidence when continuing; a new design selection should update that pointer and be recorded in Idle. Selection does not imply physical or learned-control qualification.

Never score any part of this project by binary pass/fail (design, mechanics, aesthetics, electronics, control, perception). Collect metrics as evidence, evaluate the setup qualitatively, and grade each piece of evidence on a range of how good it is; no gate, threshold or review may "call" or reject work on its own. Surface aesthetic trade-offs to the user as renders for discussion.

This is a disposable learning POC. Running the workflow is the test; do not add broad test coverage. Delete abandoned experiments when useful. Keep control learning ahead of CAD and electronics.

For the low level training campaign, the lead agent coordinates the skill tracks and assigns individual training and independent challenges to agents through Idle. Evaluate progress qualitatively from observed motion, adaptation and recovery, including how and where behavior degrades. Keep distance, timing, contact and motor measurements as evidence, not binary training scores or promotion thresholds. Preserve historical frozen scores as provenance. Count simulator transitions, optimizer updates and distinct policies separately.

Across parallel simulation, retain each full episode and aggregate continuous native-unit measurements into distributions and command-error accuracy curves by condition. Count world slots, episodes and independent start families separately. Keep early terminations and unrecovered events in view, and describe representative and severe trajectories in words rather than reducing them to a pass/fail or combined score.

Publish new training results as rows in `data/results.sqlite` with `tools/results_hub.py publish` and upload selected MP4s to the existing private Infomaniak bucket with `tools/results_hub.py upload`. The reusable viewer is `ui/results-hub/`; extend it when a display pattern recurs. Do not generate a new HTML landing page, gallery, or standalone research note for each routine training run. Keep exact native reports, traces, source/config hashes, and decisive failures as compact evidence; expire duplicate renders, abandoned variants, and superseded local videos only after the bucket copy is hash verified. See `docs/operations/results-hub.md`.

The robot learning dashboard is `http://127.0.0.1:8805/` for policy training, learning, perception, motion control and planning. Mechanical engineering, CAD and electronics design belong in the dedicated robot design dashboard at `http://127.0.0.1:8805/design/`. Both use the shared evidence database, with separate published and Archive views. Register each design study with `dashboard: "design"`, explicit `robot_sections`, one variant per stable `study_id`, retained `version` and unique result/design IDs and the complete CAD/evidence asset list. Attach measurements and simulations to the exact variant/version through `design.evidence`, or to the study through a `design_evidence` section; retain reports, configurations, full traces, source hashes and limitations. See `docs/operations/design-dashboard.md` and `tools/results_hub.py design-template --robot-section Knee`. Both dashboards prioritize human readability: concise findings, labeled native-unit measurements, CAD/motion inspection controls and expandable technical detail. Publish source images, models, and data files as `assets` on a record. New evaluators may write native reports, traces, and selected media, but must not generate a presentation HTML page or per-run gallery. Do not open a browser tab or Codex panel automatically for each run; give a stable dashboard link when a person asks to inspect. Extend the dashboard widget when a display pattern recurs. Existing unique interactive evidence may remain available inside the dashboard.
