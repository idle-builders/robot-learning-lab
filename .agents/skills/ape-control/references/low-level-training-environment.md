# Low level training environment and learning loop

Read this for a new ape primitive, a change to its MJLab/Robomimic gym, or a readiness review. The resolved run config and native results take precedence over a generic recipe. For new policy and environment reviews, use directly measured facts and qualitative behavioral judgment, never a binary or combined training score; grade each dimension separately on the 0–5 distance-to-viable scale with confidence and trend, per the [evaluation principles](../../../../docs/design/evaluation-principles.md). Historical frozen gates and GO/NO-GO reviews quoted below remain provenance. When evidence shows a gap, ask whether the environment, the policy or the requirement is wrong, and propose requirement changes to the user.

## Two assessments

**Environment readiness for bounded learning** is graded on these points: the pinned body and motor law compile, a real reset and step produce known actor/action channels, commands actually expose the required transitions, the physical outcome can be measured independently of reward, parameter draws change the intended contact/dynamics, and a short native pilot can be measured and stopped. A missing successful teacher is an explicit *data/feasibility* first stage; it is never filled with a failed trajectory. A fresh reviewer should inspect this evidence before a large retraining campaign.

Record the review **per primitive and stage**: data collection, short imitation/PPO pilot, or larger fit. The reviewer should name any track that still needs a free-object entry, observed actor goal, continuous outcome measurement or measured GPU capacity. A setup review should include source-matched evidence, per-dimension grades and the proposed next stage; it does not silently grant every track the same readiness. Reviewers grade and explain; they do not issue GO/NO-GO verdicts.

**A useful saved skill within a declared envelope** needs a reloaded learned checkpoint, independent fresh closed-loop behavior, clips that show good and degraded motion, and source-matched traces. Describe its usefulness and limitations in words alongside measured motion, contact, effort and recovery, and grade each dimension. Environment readiness, teacher motion, training reward, low imitation loss, a compiled model or a pretty video alone never imply physical transfer.

## Environment checks before compute

| Check | Evidence to keep | Concrete Cove risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body and channels | Exact XML SHA, ordered actuator joints, actor/critic observation terms and widths, action targets/offsets, first post-reset vector and zero-action step; control/physics rates. | A 21-axis count can hide a substituted body or shuffled channel. Direct Frame is an experimental control body even though the mechanical pointer no longer selects it. |
| Trainer/replay parity | Same task builder, command class, reward functions/weights, terminations, observation corruption, randomization events, actuator lag, timeout and pinned upstream runtime; actual first vector/action on the training device. | V20's evaluator had the right 73/21 widths but omitted heading reward and reset motor lag to zero. A matching shape is insufficient. |
| Entry and command | Actual `env.reset()` and predecessor states, command sampling trace, both sides/directions, planned moving→stop, upright→support, open→acquired and returned/settled phases. | Zero-command worlds may start stationary; one-foot resets do not teach the upright transition. |
| Physical behavior and reward | Whole-episode motion, contact, slip and effort in native units; source-matched video and qualitative review. Reward terms need units, intended behavior and a shortcut case. Compare reward on useful motion against parking, hopping or slipping before tuning weights, but do not turn reward or a historical gate value into the training score. | Support reward rose while the saved actor did not achieve upright foot release; pose/COM reward can pay without sole release. |
| Learning diagnostics | Per-term raw and time-scaled contribution, return/advantage, critic value loss and explained variance, KL, entropy, clip fraction, failure versus timeout. Preflight the **actual** curriculum schedule against steps per world and record final-stage exposure. | More PPO updates cannot fix an unvisited stage, wrong task signal or hidden goal. |
| Observability | Deployable actor inputs only, with capture time, frame, age, history and missing/stale handling; privileged truth may serve critic, teacher or scorer. | The legacy reach actor receives simulator-truth `ee_to_cube`; call that arm control, not image-grounded reach. |
| Effective variation | Sourced nominal/interval, sampling time, actual draws, compiled effective contact pair, coupled physical parameters and per-slice outcomes. Keep deterministic nominal separate. | Startup-only draws give one dynamics set per world; equal-priority terrain masks low sole grip. Effort-limit scale does not reproduce lower rail voltage. |
| GPU scale and spend | Import/compile smoke, warmup excluded from steady collection/update time, worlds, environment steps, memory, reset faults, wall time and posted charge. Compare outcomes at equal total simulator steps. | 4096 **worlds** may train one actor, not 4096 policies; camera/contact tasks may saturate before gait tasks. |
| Headless observability | If an original needs video or camera input, install and attest the Linux GLVND EGL dispatch loader (`libegl1` on Ubuntu 24.04), expose NVIDIA graphics/EGL capability, set the backend before any MuJoCo import, and run the exact task's render plus encode/decode path on a fresh unscored probe. Keep the OS package and shared-library inventory, frame, MP4 and resolved launch environment. | V35 A40-r2 passed a physics-only CUDA sentinel, then failed at `mujoco.Renderer` because `MUJOCO_GL` was unset. R3 set it and exposed the NVIDIA EGL vendor but failed before import because generic `libEGL.so.1` was missing. Neither a Python package closure nor a vendor library alone establishes a working video path. |

For native evaluation, measure **valid source-checked families per GPU-hour** as well as training steps/s. V34's fresh Python/CUDA process took about 98 s per ten-second route end to end; the throughput proposal describes a future persistent process. Adopt it only after exact CUDA, reset/noise/history and matched-start parity against the serial reference. Before marking an original attempt, reserve enough wall time for a full route and output flush; a truncated route stays recorded as truncated, never silently replaced.

Pin the **actual initial state**, not just the checkpoint and body. The [V3b locomotion preflight](../../../../artifacts/ape-cove50-locomotion-learning-v3-20261014/preflight-v3b/README.md) strictly reloaded the saved PPO state but found its trainer reset root at 0.231 m while historical play used the generic 0.181 m default. Make the prospective primary entry explicit and score alternate entry heights as a separate sensitivity slice. A common seed does not equate different reset-event streams; compare captured qpos/qvel and first observation/action under matched CUDA sentinels.

Stress resets at the **intended GPU world count** before PPO updates, including many explicit resets and automatic partial resets under initial exploration-scale actions. Check active contact slots and both collision-buffer capacities, bounded independent retry counts, finite actor/reward/action/pose tensors, first-step zero-action terminations, and saved reset poses against native MuJoCo. A small CPU reset sample is insufficient: the V22 seated stage passed 64 local entries, then V23 exhausted its reset on a 2,048-world CUDA pilot after two PPO iterations. Keep that as an environment failure; no learned checkpoint was scored. The repaired V24 reset passed a frozen 2,048-world CUDA stress with 418,952 accepted resets, including 384,136 automatic partial resets, zero recorded reset/capacity/nonfinite/first-step faults, and 0/196 deep overlaps in a separate bare-body pose audit. Fresh shared-task references passed 6/6 full rises versus 0/6 parked half-rises. Two independent reviews cleared one capped diagnostic seated-rise PPO pilot, V25, which is now stopped with 0/6 controlled planted stands. The 6,180 accepted >15 mm predicted sole-gap entries remain a sealed robustness slice because no candidate cleared the development gate. A reset repair changes the training distribution, so repeat reward/physical reference checks before optimization.

For each reward candidate, calculate every term on at least one physically successful trace and one shortcut trace from the same lesson. Additive pose and progress terms can pay a policy that never satisfies the required contact or transition. Keep the physical outcome measurement outside the training reward. The PPO critic predicts reward return, so compare its values with measured returns and diagnose poor fit; separately check whether those returns rank physically successful episodes above shortcuts. Do not treat the critic as a direct physical-success predictor. [Isaac Lab's reward exploration walkthrough](https://isaac-sim.github.io/IsaacLab/main/source/setup/walkthrough/training_jetbot_reward_exploration.html) demonstrates how plausible additive terms admit unintended behavior; copy its diagnosis method, not its reward formula.

The [V30 first-original failure](../../../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/seated-v30-first24/report.json) adds a native runtime check: freezing top-level MJLab, MuJoCo, RSL-RL and Torch versions did not pin `warp-lang`; pip selected 1.18.0 and MJLab 1.3.0 failed at CUDA Simulation construction before reset. Pin the installed dependency closure, including Warp and Torch/Triton, then **construct the actual CUDA task on a disjoint diagnostic sentinel before any frozen original**. Record `pip check`, first actor/action and device model. A package import, GPU query or source-hash comparison alone does not show that the task constructs. A crashed original stays recorded as crashed; do not relabel it as a smoke test afterward.

The [locomotion CUDA entry v1 failure](../../../../artifacts/ape-cove50-locomotion-cuda-entry-v1-20261014/report.json) exposed a different source-closure fault. MJLab's `ape_walk` task entrypoint eagerly registered unrelated tasks; `foot_upright` read right/left endpoint JSON during import, though the planned evaluator targeted gait. The worker bundle omitted those files, and the installed wheel resolved its repository root as `/root`; the captured traceback then shows wheel-origin `ape_walk/task.py` and a duplicate `Mjlab-Velocity-Flat-Ape` registration. The pod stopped with zero sentinels, zero opened episodes and zero optimizer updates. Before a fresh worker import, close the entrypoint's **import-time** asset dependencies as well as target-task files, set the exact checkout-first `PYTHONPATH`, and assert `find_spec("ape_walk")` selects that checkout before import. After import, verify the eager modules' origins and hashes. The [captured worker traceback](../../../../artifacts/ape-cove50-locomotion-audit-20261014/cuda-launch/attempt-v1-runtime-failure.log) is the evidence for this specific failure; the new v2 package remains unrun.

For recovery, the [2026 sit-to-stand PPO study](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20823v1) is a useful mechanism example: physically valid varied initial poses, 50 Hz proprioceptive control, rise-stage rewards, explicit safety terminations and a separate force-free physical score. Its chair, assistance force, reward weights and reported rate are not a Cove floor-squat recipe. The Cove [V8 native audit](../../../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/reward-counterfactual-v8/report.json) ranked one success above one fall overall, yet the fallen trace received **+41.54** after 1 s, positive on all 450 later steps because pose/arm terms remained payable. A reward-ranking claim must include whole return **and** post-failure behavior, with actual failure termination and terminal metrics; merely increasing upright weight is not a complete repair.

Check what each contact sensor actually matches after scene assembly. A robot subtree matched against itself detects self-contact, **not** terrain impact. The seated recovery V5 trace wrongly reported no body contact on two fallen starts; full-qpos MuJoCo replay found floor–pelvis contact from 0.86/0.88 s, and [V6](../../../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/contact-sensor-parity-v6/report.json) verified the corrected terrain-to-nonsole-body sensor frame for frame without changing the physical trajectory. Double-sole flags can reappear while prone with negligible force. Recovery evidence needs upright/height and forbidden terrain contact as well as stance contact; use the full 50 Hz trace, not a final pose or `ended_early` when fall terminations are disabled.

Use pinned upstream mechanisms: [MJLab environment configuration](https://mujocolab.github.io/mjlab/main/source/environment_config.html), [events](https://mujocolab.github.io/mjlab/main/source/events.html), [metrics](https://mujocolab.github.io/mjlab/main/source/metrics.html), [randomization](https://mujocolab.github.io/mjlab/main/source/randomization.html) and [RSL-RL training](https://mujocolab.github.io/mjlab/main/source/training/rsl_rl.html). Copy their mechanism after checking the installed revision and assembled scene, not their world count, reward weights or claimed speed.

For a staged recovery curriculum, log how many environment steps each reset/command stage receives, not just its authored schedule. Use physically valid reset states, actor-visible stage/phase if actions depend on it, and disjoint start families for evaluation. The [V9 opened-start phase probe](../../../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/phase-alignment-v9/report.json) preserved four successes but rescued neither fall, so changing the scripted controller's initial blend is not an established learning fix. A hidden phase used by an imitation teacher is still an actor-interface mismatch even if the script's first action looks reasonable.

## Demonstration and variation decisions

Collect **whole native episodes** from actual resets or attained predecessor states. Keep attempted and retained counts, source body/motor law, entry family, task phase, terminal physical outcome, reasons for excluding episodes and motor/contact feasibility. Split by independent trajectory family, not adjacent frames. [DeepMimic](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.02717) supports reference-plus-task training; [MimicGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17596) expands successful manipulation paths; [DAgger](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a.html) requires a competent teacher on learner-visited states. A time-indexed teacher that cannot recover a slipped object cannot supply corrective labels there.

A trajectory family includes its reset seed and state distribution, not only its friction or command setting. Holding out a grip value while reusing the same deterministic reset seed is an interpolation check, not independent-start validation. Before any closed-loop BC score, reconstruct the **full native action vector** on a teacher state and compare each channel's action and joint target. An inactive learned channel may still need a nonzero native action to cancel a configured offset and hold the teacher pose. Freeze that baseline explicitly; a low loss on the few predicted channels cannot validate the omitted channels. Then test the learned actor on independent perturbed starts with full physical measurements, and stop early once the evidence shows the planned outcome is out of reach. The [Stage-A support BC pilot](../../../../artifacts/ape-low-level-training-20261013/support-load-bc-pilot-v2/stopped-report.json) exposed both errors.

Count physical reset families, not left/right episodes, as independent examples when both commands start from the same seed. If fresh replay includes high reset disturbance, include that disturbance in training and reserve other whole high-disturbance starts for validation; otherwise label the result extrapolation. A phase or command-age feature must be available from the deployed skill-entry clock, and its schedule limits the claim. Compare it against the same matched phase-only teacher: imitation of a fixed timed motion is not evidence of corrective feedback. The Stage-A 18-episode teacher ledger and frozen phase-BC method show the bounded split and the pre-replay first-actor/all-action smoke.

When a seed-disjoint BC still fails fresh closed loop, identify the **first failed phase** before adding more demonstrations or epochs. The Cove V6 actor had a small average validation loss but fell during stand, before its support command; forcing exact teacher stand actions made the first second match in every saved state/action channel, then the unchanged BC failed during the requested load shift. The [fresh adversarial challenge](../../../../artifacts/ape-low-level-training-20261013/support-phase-bc-v6/independent-challenge.md) therefore stops further time-only BC fits. A timed teacher cannot supply corrective [DAgger](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a.html) labels on a fallen learner state. For a reference-guided on-policy pilot, implement the reference-plus-residual native action adapter explicitly and prove a **deterministic no-sampling zero-residual** full-episode parity, including all 21 actions, clipping, both sides and return. The present support trainer emits absolute actions and its stochastic PPO exploration does not satisfy this check. An episode side that disappears from the current command during return must be retained through a deployable skill-state latch. Check that the **full** candidate reward ranks successful traces above shortcut traces; use a final-step physical success metric (`reduce="last"` in [MJLab metrics](https://mujocolab.github.io/mjlab/main/source/metrics.html)) for reporting and a separate reward term if PPO should earn a terminal bonus. Confirm full slip, motor-duty and contact accumulation at the actual terminal step. Inspect critic as well as actor diagnostics. Choose GPU worlds from measured task throughput, not a copied number.

For that support pilot, do not reuse old teacher traces to claim complete reward ranking when they lack raw inherited reward or contact fields. The resolved load lesson still pays inherited COM, grounded-stance, stillness and side-tilt terms; instrument **all** terms on matched successful and failed native runs. Inspect both the authored target's 1–2 s ramp/5–7 s return and the command manager's slower `alpha` ramp. A three-second partial hold counter does not measure the physical outcome, which needs every frame in the 2–5 s load window, bounded whole-episode sole slip and motor duty, clean contact and stable return.

When an evaluator writes a command directly between MJLab steps, check the actor's recorded command fields at reset, request onset and return. In pinned MJLab 1.3.0, `get_observations()` can return the previous cached buffer. The current support diagnostic uses `observation_manager.compute_group("actor", update_history=False)` for a fresh actor read after the command write; verify that the task has no actor history/delay before using that shortcut. A one-frame stale command or random reset-time command can poison the first action even when the teacher trace later looks correct.

Start with the closest valid native demonstration or reference. Fit a short imitation candidate and test it **closed loop** against saved policy and zero action before paying for a long RL run. If no full teacher exists, first measure which phase is feasible and why the teacher fails. PPO remains the pinned baseline for feedback learning; alternatives need a matched failure hypothesis, equal body/steps/seeds/measurements and an explicit budget.

The current seated reference is a concrete stop example: [V4](../../../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/seat-command-development-v4/report.json) showed at most 3.8 mm post-anchor sole-center motion despite 4.5–5.9 cm necessary root travel, so the old 5 cm absolute-root gate was a poor slip measure: the requirement, not only the solution, was wrong. A corrected physical gate was frozen before [V5](../../../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/seat-command-development-v5/report.json): 4/6 fresh seated starts passed and two fell. The predeclared zero-fall teacher rule failed. Independent review found the script uses hidden internal phase, lacks pitch/contact feedback and still earns positive step reward while prone. Do not select the successful subset after the fact and quietly call the same pool independent BC validation. Use opened failures for diagnosis, freeze a corrective teacher and a new disjoint score before claiming a demonstration bank or paying for fit.

For contact BC, audit both the label and the actor's next input. A zero-scale actuator may still feed a raw previous-action feature; clamp such channels structurally in the output contract. Check whether the teacher reacts to simulator-truth contact, object pose or another signal absent from the actor. An open-loop action MAE can fall while closed-loop contact fails. The current one-pad touch diagnosis shows both failure modes on fresh native starts; its next step is a competent deployable-feedback teacher and physical replay, not more epochs on the same labels.

Choose parameter ranges from a physical source or label them sensitivity hypotheses. Verify low/high endpoints in the compiled training contact scene, sample at reset when episode diversity is intended, log actual draws, and score nominal, each axis and held combinations separately. Preserve coherent mass/inertia, actuator/voltage and timing combinations. Widen a range after observing its failure boundary; do not equate a broad authored range with robustness. [Automatic Domain Randomization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.07113) is one optional expansion method, not a requirement to copy its hyperparameters.

## One short cycle

Freeze baseline, hypothesis, teacher ledger, command/reset distribution, reward shortcut check, independent development and untouched historical score families, observed physical outcome to describe, and spend cap. Verify the full installed package closure and `pip check` after all installs; check imported Torch/Triton/Warp origins, then construct, reset and step the **exact CUDA task** on a disjoint unscored sentinel before any original or optimizer update. Run a small pilot; inspect term/critic diagnostics and synchronized smooth, rough and severe-failure traces. Replay the saved checkpoint in a fresh native process. Decide in words whether it is a useful next baseline and grade each dimension 0–5 for distance to viable, with measured distance, duration, contact, effort and recovery beside the judgment. Do not calculate a binary or combined score. After two uninformative *discriminating* cycles on one hypothesis, obtain a new adversarial reviewer and read primary literature/upstream code before paying for another variant. Preserve failures and stop paid compute when the bounded run ends. For a paid pod, account for setup and verification as billable time; arm an independent stop guard before launch, verify it is active, and report whether its limit is local or provider enforced. The V30 independent re-review shows why a source/import GO was insufficient: its first original crashed at task construction under unpinned Warp, leaving zero usable pairs. The later failure supersedes that narrow historical GO.

If the frozen GPU SKU is unavailable, do not substitute another GPU under the same plan. Before any original on the alternative hardware, freeze a separate plan, runtime and cost guard; verify actual GPU identity, package closure and an unscored native sentinel. A full-duration route with video on an already opened training seed can measure the new workload's wall time only if its frame count does not depend on an untested actor surviving to the end. Count and decode the full expected frame sequence, record termination/reset counts, and keep the actor's separate physical result separate. Capture bootstrap, graphics-attestation and worker stdout/stderr plus exit codes before stopping the pod, so a pre-visit failure remains diagnosable. Compare fresh routes only against matched baselines on that GPU, label the hardware change and keep independent score families sealed. See the V35 seated imitation method for why CPU action loss and old GPU trajectories cannot stand in for a native policy score.

Treat a frozen paid packet as **one-use after its first pod creation**, even when no scored trajectory completes. A stop and restart must not reload the zero-attempt ledger embedded in its original worker archive. Bind the canonical consumed ledger and first failure evidence in a new packet; do not arm it if the old packet already has a pod attestation or any attempt. A prelaunch dry-run score is not a current result after a native failure: retain it under an explicit prelaunch name and publish a failure summary from the worker ledger and log.

The [V3e locomotion guard abort](../../../../artifacts/ape-cove50-locomotion-learning-v3-20261014/preflight-v3e-diagnosis-20261014/INCIDENT-REVIEW.md) adds a transport check to that rule. An independently reviewed static stop guard still aborted before any CUDA step because its 30-second attestation could expire while five separate files were relayed. For a live provider watchdog, measure attestation-to-worker arrival and polling delay under healthy and deliberately stalled delivery. Send one atomic, source-bound lease containing the checked provider state; preserve immutable creation evidence separately. Log the exact failed predicate and remote snapshot on abort. Choose freshness from measured delivery plus margin while retaining the paid deadline and stop reserve. Do not fix a stale lease by extending its timeout alone, and never relabel a consumed setup attempt as a native training result.

Publish each cycle through the shared inspector. A high level planner is a later consumer of the measured skill outcomes.
