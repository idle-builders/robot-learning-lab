# Ape experiments

Locate `robot-learning-lab` by its `AGENTS.md`; work there. Read the active goal and relevant Idle tree state before choosing work. Use the installed idle skill/tools with an explicit `by`; search existing tasks and decisions before adding work. A planning request does not itself authorize running all experiments or provisioning compute.

Plan storage as part of the experiment: keep active inputs and compact provenance local, and move useful large completed evidence to the existing private Infomaniak blob archive with verified recovery before eviction. Read the [retention and restore guide](../../docs/design/artifact-storage.md) before closing large runs or reusing cold inputs. Local deduplication is not cloud archival; restore required paths/dependencies before native enumeration or export, and keep generated bulk out of Git.

The target is the **50 cm ape**. Historical 35 cm/22-axis policies and G1/Berkeley/Microduck/Microban results are references. Check the selected body's actual axes: current 21-axis intent and 19/14-axis comparisons are not a frozen embodiment with graded physical evidence. Keep control learning ahead of CAD/electronics, and derive mechanical questions from a task failure or capability need.

For the active control-first campaign, use [the Cove-50 low level training program](../../docs/research/ape-low-level-training-program-20261013.md), its [2026 primary-source review](../../docs/research/ape-rl-best-practice-2026.md), and the [current independent per-skill stage matrix](../../docs/research/ape-low-level-independent-readiness-20261014.md). The Direct Frame source named by the mechanical pointer is an experimental training body; unresolved mechanical selection does not block low level learning on it. Keep trained simulation outcomes distinct from later physical evidence. Structure Idle work as parallel primitive tracks with only genuine prerequisites, and keep high level planning outside the skill-training scope.

For simulation-world decisions, read [the 16 October world study](../../docs/research/ape-low-level-simulation-world-20261016.md). Compare the exact Cove world with a working upstream humanoid recipe and trace motor/contact, perturbation recovery, camera geometry and *useful* sustained experience per GPU hour. Do not equate photorealism, raw transitions/s or thousands of parallel worlds with improved low level control. New development reviews use directly measured facts, qualitative judgment in words and per-dimension 0–5 distance-to-viable grades ([evaluation principles](../../docs/design/evaluation-principles.md)); historical binary gates remain provenance.
For the immediate reuse experiment, use [the model-compatible dojo plan](../../docs/research/ape-model-compatible-dojo-plan-20261016.md): reproduce a released controller's **inference** in its pinned native environment, then bring Cove into that model's actual contract and compare zero-update motion. Copy sourced details rather than designing a generic gym by assumption. Disclose robot-specific differences instead of copying another body's motor or mass values. Put no new G1 training ahead of this comparison.
For thousands of parallel rollouts, use [the measurement aggregation contract](../../docs/research/ape-low-level-measurement-aggregation-20261016.md): report continuous tracking-error accuracy curves and native-unit distributions by condition and independent family, with direct event counts and reviewed source clips. Do not convert world slots or repeated resets into independent policy counts, or bury short falls in a frame-weighted average.

## Find the right evidence and workflow

Read `docs/research/ape-learning-roadmap-20261006.md` for the learning synthesis, task map and source precedence, then only the relevant records:

For a learning-material claim or a new evaluation design, read [evidence and evaluation](references/evidence-and-evaluation.md). `docs/research/learning-audit-20261007/README.md` indexes the detailed section audit and corrections; knowledge belongs in reusable guidance, while an unproven improvement for this ape belongs in a falsifiable Idle experiment.

- Policy training, replay, contact manipulation or composition: [ape-control](../ape-control/SKILL.md); `skills/ape/registry.json` is the **robot policy shelf**, distinct from these Codex instructions.
- Image-grounded teaching, finding, belief, gaze or sensor experiments: [ape-perception](../ape-perception/SKILL.md).
- Geometry, joint allocation, actuator/contact assumptions or transfer to a changed body: [ape-body-transfer](../ape-body-transfer/SKILL.md).
- A specifically requested G1/Berkeley Arena/GR00T reference: [train-arena-groot](../train-arena-groot/SKILL.md). It is not the ape training default.
- Runpod provisioning or cleanup: use the installed runpod skill. Develop locally; use the 10 GB Runpod volume only for bounded worker transfer and retrieve results. A stopped request is not proof a remote job stopped. The deleted 320 GB and 235 GB volumes are historical provenance.

## Turn a question into a discriminating experiment

State the capability being improved, the current failure evidence, and one hypothesis. Choose the cheapest comparison that can change a decision. Examples: unchanged policy on old/new body; same images with different identity gate; synchronous versus delayed actions with the same policy. Avoid a new framework when existing upstream trainers and repository probes cover the question.

Before running, write into the Idle task's plan:

- Baseline and changed factor; exact body, checkpoint, controller and data identities.
- What the actor actually observes and emits: frames, units, timing, normalization, history/phase, privileged training inputs and deployable inputs.
- Training/development cases versus held-out episodes/scenes; independent physical or interaction outcome, failure/abstention measures and a negative control where it discriminates.
- A bounded run and stopping condition; the evidence that would lead you to continue, change direction or retain the baseline (a judgement, not an automatic cutoff). Choose reference values for this task, not from a generic prep document, and record where each comes from so it can be challenged. Declare the regression size you would consider meaningful and the sampling before comparison; a nonsignificant adverse result is not proof of no regression.
- Valid demonstration provenance and the teacher's physical success from actual reset/attained states; reward terms with physical meaning and shortcut checks; per-world parameter intervals with source; world-count/throughput benchmark and attributable compute budget.

For camera or video work on a Linux worker, include OS graphics libraries in the frozen runtime inventory as well as Python distributions. On Ubuntu 24.04, attest generic `libEGL.so.1` from `libegl1` and `libOpenGL.so.0` from `libopengl0`, NVIDIA vendor libraries, `MUJOCO_GL=egl` before import, and a live NVIDIA context. Then decode a nonblank MP4 from the exact task before opening an original. A provider graphics flag or Python `pip check` cannot establish this path alone.

Organize each skill into short smoke, pilot and fresh replay cycles. Track development and frozen independent evidence separately; grade progress against prospective, skill-specific reference values rather than demanding that every case succeed. After two uninformative discriminating cycles, get a fresh adversarial diagnosis and check primary papers/upstream code before spending on another variant. Record the resulting hypothesis change in Idle. Preparation and evaluation for unrelated skills can run concurrently; on a single GPU, sequence training jobs to avoid contention.

The current [support example](../../docs/research/ape-support-reference-residual-design-20261014.md) shows this stop rule: a scripted teacher passed 18/18 episodes from nine independent physical starts while two imitation candidates failed fresh closed loop. The independent challenge and exact first-second counterfactual changed the next question to reward-checked feedback learning. A [fresh independent method review](../../docs/research/ape-support-residual-independent-review-20261014.md) then blocked paid PPO on missing action, command-clock, complete reward and terminal-gate contracts. [V10 parity](../../docs/research/ape-support-reference-parity-review-20261014.md) independently cleared only deterministic same-CPU zero-residual equivalence (4/4 opened pairs, zero action/state gap). Preserve the working teacher as a reference baseline and the BC attempts as failure evidence; neither an action-interface proof nor more teacher frames justifies paid feedback training by itself. The [posture Stage-A screen](../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/stage-a-20261014/report.json) similarly shows 0/12 retained-actor physical passes and no accepted recovery teacher, so collect successful native recovery before fitting it.

The [seated V1–V5 sequence](../../docs/research/ape-cove50-seated-stance-and-fresh-score-20261014.md) shows why the requirement can be wrong as well as the solution: three opened stance traces had under 3.8 mm sole-center motion while a 5 cm root-distance gate rejected two; the revised gate was frozen before six fresh starts and found **4/6 scripted successes plus two falls**. Its zero-fall reference requirement failed. The [independent V5 review](../../docs/research/ape-cove50-seated-v5-independent-review-20261014.md) also found a missing floor-body contact sensor, a hidden script phase and reward paid while prone. The corrected sensor is source-matched, but no recovery teacher or learned result is adopted. Keep old root-distance scores intact and use new independent starts only after a corrective method is frozen.

On those opened seated starts, [V8](../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/reward-counterfactual-v8/report.json) measured **+41.54** post-fall reward on 450/450 positive steps; [V9](../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/phase-alignment-v9/report.json) retained four passes but rescued **0/2** falls by changing internal initial phase. Treat both as diagnosis, not new validation. At that V9 point, the next recovery environment needed physical fall/contact termination and complete success/no-op/fall reward evidence before a paid learned pilot; the later separate seated stage is described below. Record the closed hypothesis and independent review in its existing Idle task; do not relabel V5 seeds as fresh.

The subsequent [V16 seated-only entry](../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/seated-rise-environment-v16/report.json) cleared 64/64 reset/interface checks, and [V17](../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/seated-rise-native-v17/report.json) produced 6/6 scripted development passes versus 0/6 matched zero-action. [V18](../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/seated-rise-reward-v18/report.json) challenged that score with an equally long parked half-rise: reward preferred full rise 6/6, but the old height band falsely passed all six half-rises. [V20](../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/seated-rise-randomized-v20/report.json) froze a ≥0.22 m check and measured 5/6 full rises versus 0/6 parked, but later source audit found a different reward task and lost motor-delay variation. The corrected shared builder and [V22 review](../../docs/research/ape-cove50-seated-trainer-parity-20261014.md) cleared one capped diagnostic PPO pilot after **6/6** full rises, **0/6** parked passes and a named zero-action fall. V23 then exposed a 2,048-world CUDA reset failure after two pilot iterations; no checkpoint was scored. The [V24 full-scale repair](../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/seated-rise-reset-repair-v24/report.json) passed 418,952 CUDA resets, 0/196 sampled native deep overlaps and 6/6 fresh full-rise versus 0/6 parked references. Two independent reviews permitted the V25 capped diagnostic PPO pilot, with separate scoring of the >15 mm sole-gap tail; V25 ran and did not meet its historical planted-stand gate. Treat all these as **reference/environment** results, not an adopted learned policy. Freeze the exact trainer/evaluator task, reward, command, corruption, lag and runtime before paid fitting; judge critic, physical outcome and compute qualitatively at the pilot's planned stop.

The [V25 pilot](../../docs/research/ape-cove50-seated-v25-learner-20261014.md) used that clearance and stopped after **31 updates / 1.52 million steps**: six of six saved-actor development runs stayed upright for 10 s, but **zero of six** met the planted-foot physical gate. All six had foot relocation and support gaps; three exceeded rated duty. Its higher reward and survival are useful motion discovery, not a controlled rise. The source-checked [V26 exact CUDA comparison](../../docs/research/ape-cove50-seated-v26-cuda-feasibility-20261014.md) found **6/6** planted scripted passes versus **0/6** learned passes on matched opened starts, with exact learned replay parity. The [V27 offline audit](../../docs/research/ape-cove50-seated-v27-first-divergence-20261014.md) located identical first actor observations, first-step action/target divergence, height attainment at 0.12 s and support loss at 0.16–0.18 s for model25. Existing full, early and discounted reward all rank the script above the hop. The [V28 actor audit](../../docs/research/ape-cove50-seated-v28-actor-exploration-20261014.md) reconstructed saved first actions; the [V29 live CUDA intervention](../../docs/research/ape-cove50-seated-v29-intervention-20261014.md) ran 30 source-checked visits with zero optimizer steps: actor→script at 0.10 s passed 4/6 planted gates, while actor→script at 0.20 s and script→actor at 0.20 s each passed 0/6. The script has a narrow correction window and cannot be treated as a general learner-state teacher. The next discrimination is actor-visible early warning versus phase-only input, varied whole-episode demonstration validity and complete proposed tracking-reward ranking on planted/hop/park/fall. Do not start another seated optimizer fit from a missing-penalty story alone. Preserve the untouched 12-start reserve and check any proposed reward's actual command/stage activation.

For learned skills, specify supported entry states, command range, outcome and stop/recovery conditions. A planner's completion text or a policy timeout cannot verify possession, placement, stopping or object identity. Compose on states actually reached by the predecessor, without resetting physics or silently changing control coordinates.

## Classify results before recording them

The [V30 diagnostic collection](../../artifacts/ape-cove50-stand-recovery/seated-v30-first24/report.json) is **closed failed**: one frozen original crashed before native reset, with **0/24 valid pairs, 0 native trajectories and 0 optimizer updates** ($0.147 listed GPU estimate). Preserve failed seed33001 and the old-host capacity failures. V31/V31b were not launched; V31c failed before construction on a missing actuator catalog ($0.1064 estimate). The [V31d sentinel](../../docs/research/ape-cove50-native-runtime-v31d-20261014.md) passed the exact seated CUDA constructor/reset/one step on disjoint seed34000 with 17 source files and 227 installed distributions verified ($0.1002 estimate); independent final review gave a **narrow staged-environment GO**. These are elapsed-rate estimates, not verified posted charges; V31c/d used no serverless. V31d produced **0 demonstrations, 0 scored originals and 0 optimizer updates**. Only whole native episodes reviewed as physically valid may enter a later imitation bank; multiworld readiness, reward and PPO stages each need their own evidence.

Distinguish a proposal, static/source inspection, offline replay, fresh closed-loop simulation, and physical measurement. Label scripted references, BC/imitation, reward-updated RL and memory edits accurately. A saved checkpoint, model load, plausible video, detector box, scalar reward or low loss is not the intended task outcome.

Then grade each relevant dimension (for example motion quality, contact and support, motor duty, robustness across conditions, recovery, interface completeness) from 0 to 5 for distance to viable, with confidence and trend, using the reporting template in the [evaluation principles](../../docs/design/evaluation-principles.md). Do not average the grades. When a result falls short of a reference value, ask whether the solution or the requirement is wrong (where the number came from, what it buys, what it costs), record both, and propose any requirement change to the user instead of relaxing or enforcing it silently.

Keep source/config/model hashes, seed and split, exact evaluated body, observation/action meanings, metrics and inspectable failed as well as successful episodes. Use existing `lab.artifact_store` and run manifests rather than a new artifact system. Retain the selected replayable policy, baseline and compact decisive evidence; expire unselected weights, caches and redundant raw arrays after review. Archive small policies in the existing private Hugging Face archive or selected large files to the existing Infomaniak S3 archive with a SHA-256 verified restore; follow [the storage plan](../../docs/operations/storage-retention-plan-20261013.md). Inspect native metrics alongside synchronized video/traces. If a required check cannot run, report `not_run` with its missing prerequisite; do not substitute a different kind of evidence.

The [ape-inspector](../ape-inspector/SKILL.md) routes policy, perception, motion-control and planning results to `http://127.0.0.1:8805/`, and CAD/engineering studies to the separate section-based design dashboard at `http://127.0.0.1:8805/design/`, with selected videos in the existing private bucket. Publish compact records through that flow; keep authoritative native reports, traces and source hashes as evidence. Extend a reusable widget when a display pattern recurs. Do not ask agents to make per-run web pages or open a new tab or Codex panel for each result.

Use task feedback for what changed the next decision. Update existing unfinished goals instead of multiplying duplicate task lists; retain completed historical evidence and active claims. New exploration ideas outside accepted scope start `proposed`. A current roadmap link is useful; copying the whole live task backend into docs creates drift.
