# Ape joint design bank

Work locally in `robot-learning-lab`. The 50 cm ape is the target. The [bank guide](../../studies/cove50-joint-bank/README.md), [index](../../studies/cove50-joint-bank/INDEX.md), [record format](../../studies/cove50-joint-bank/RECORD-FORMAT.md), and `records/*.json` are the working contract; the [morphology overview](../../studies/cove50-morphology/JOINT-DESIGN-BANK.md) explains the research choices. Read the relevant Idle task and decision **D-6bhbcz** before changing scope. This phase delivers a reusable bank of options, not a selected body or a physical assessment of every joint.

Keep compact bank records, source identities/hashes and active source parts local; store useful large inactive geometry packages and completed motion/CAD evidence in the existing private Infomaniak blob archive. Follow the [retention and restore guide](../../docs/design/artifact-storage.md) before eviction or retrieval: verify recovery before removing local bytes and restore each required asset and dependency before `resolve`, `stage` or native CAD use. Preserve logical paths and hashes in records; keep generated bulk out of Git and avoid duplicating the entire bank into each study.

## Retrieve an option for design work

Use `python3 studies/cove50-joint-bank/bank.py` to `list` by region, architecture, maturity, CAD, or motion availability; `show <ID>` for the full record; `motion <ID>` for normalized saved poses; and `resolve <ID> <asset>` for a verified source path. Inspect `limits`, source documentation, interface frame, units, and any failed trial before reuse. `source_only` means there is no banked CAD; a missing origin is unknown, not zero. A saved pose is neither continuous collision clearance nor a control trajectory.

Stage a useful candidate with `stage <ID> --out <new-directory>`. Add `--with-shell` only when the selected exterior STEP helps the CAD task. The staged `package.json` retains source hashes and frame information; local specimen parts are not automatically aligned to that shell. Keep generated or modified geometry separate from the frozen source package and identify its parameters and provenance.

## Complete or maintain the bank

When asked to complete it, first check whether a specific CAD or motion user action is blocked: finding an option, interpreting its interface, retrieving a part, or comparing a saved motion result. Repair that gap in the structured record, source asset, or tool. Do not reopen broad motor cataloging or mechanism discovery merely because more possibilities exist. Grade bank completeness on the 0–5 distance-to-viable scale in the [evaluation principles](../../docs/design/evaluation-principles.md), using these reference points: each anatomical region has retrievable alternatives or an explicit source-only gap, records state their reuse limits, and representative CAD and motion packages can be staged and inspected. If a reference point looks wrong for the CAD or motion work it serves, say so and propose a change to the user rather than silently dropping or enforcing it.

For a new or corrected entry, follow `RECORD-FORMAT.md`: retain exact source identity, maturity and failure evidence; name actual parent/output interfaces and supported degrees of freedom; pin asset paths and SHA-256; state CAD units and frames; and leave unsupported placement, load, thermal, tolerance, or motion claims unknown. Link existing STEP/GLB pieces, editable parameters and generator, documentation, and motion reports where they exist. Do not fabricate missing geometry or present a bench/local trial as physical readiness. Run `bank.py validate`, regenerate with `bank.py index`, then retrieve and stage the affected entry to verify the user workflow. Inspect representative geometry or motion output when the change affects its interpretation. The running workflow is the test; avoid broad test scaffolding for this disposable POC.

The selected shell pointer is `robot/ape-current-shell.json`; its STEP is an exterior datum with visual-only limits. `robot/ape-current-design.json` controls any selected mechanical design. Bank curation does not change that pointer. For integration into a changed physics body, use [ape-body-transfer](../ape-body-transfer/SKILL.md); for policy training or motion evaluation, use [ape-control](../ape-control/SKILL.md). Carry forward an option's measured failures and unknowns instead of implying that a CAD file proves ape fit or motion capability.

When a bank option produces a reviewed CAD or motion comparison, keep the bank record as source evidence and publish the comparison through [ape-inspector](../ape-inspector/SKILL.md) to the dedicated robot design dashboard with `dashboard: "design"`, `workspace: "Body"` and explicit `robot_sections`, with native-unit measurements, a written judgement and per-dimension grades (for example fit, drive, mass, buildability) rather than a pass/fail verdict. Show aesthetic trade-offs (bulges, recesses, seams, cover shapes) to the user as renders for discussion. Attach selected images, complete-scene GLBs and native reports as assets. Reuse the dashboard instead of making a new comparison page or opening a tab for that run.

Follow [design-study registration](../../docs/operations/design-dashboard.md) when presenting banked CAD: one variant per stable `study_id`, retained `version` and unique result/design IDs, source-matched GLB/STEP/joint metadata in `assets` and `cad`, and exact-version `design.evidence` items for measurements or simulations. Retain conditions, native units, source/configuration hashes, full traces, limitations and registered recording IDs. `tools/results_hub.py design-template --robot-section Knee` emits the maintained authoring format. Bank source-only records remain source-only; do not invent CAD or motion to fill dashboard fields.
