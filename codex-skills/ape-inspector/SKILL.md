# Ape experiment dashboard

Work in `robot-learning-lab`. Start both dashboards with `.venv/bin/python tools/results_hub.py serve`:
- **Learning:** `http://127.0.0.1:8805/` for policy training, perception, motion control and planning.
- **Robot design:** `http://127.0.0.1:8805/design/` for CAD, mechanism exploration, engineering measurements and design simulations, browsed by robot section.

Both share `data/results.sqlite` and the publisher, with separate published and Archive views. Use `dashboard: "design"` and explicit `robot_sections` for engineering; Body/CAD publications default to design. Policy/body-transfer behavior remains learning. Old design links redirect. Port 8804 only redirects old links. Publish without opening a browser tab or Codex panel; give the correct dashboard link when inspection is requested.

Read the [retention, archive and restore guide](../../docs/design/artifact-storage.md) when publishing large completed evidence or inspecting cold assets. Keep compact result/source/config/hash records local and retained bulk in the existing private Infomaniak blob archive; selected videos still use `tools/results_hub.py upload`. Preserve the complete logical asset inventory and version/source identity after eviction. The dashboard retrieves indexed cold assets on demand; native tools and filesystem enumeration need explicit restoration of the required paths/dependencies. Verify recovery before eviction, keep generated bulk out of Git, and remember that `.artifact-store` only deduplicates locally.

For CAD or design-study registration, read [the design publishing contract](../../docs/operations/design-dashboard.md). Run `tools/results_hub.py design-template --robot-section Knee`. A **study is exactly one variant**, with a stable `study_id`; each iteration has a `version`, unique result `id`, and one `design` object. A robot section contains several studies. Different mechanisms are separate studies, not versions of a designer lane. Opening a study shows its latest version; older evidence remains in its version menu. Section preferences select a study, not a version or whole robot. Register the version's complete GLB/STEP/render/joint/report inventory in `assets` and `design.cad`. Preserve frames, units, source axes, ranges and couplings; static exports remain labeled static. The shared viewer provides joint motion, shells, cuts and display-only explosion. Body-map hotspots and the text section list are synchronized.

Attach this variant/version's results in `design.evidence`; attach additional version-specific results in a `design_evidence` section's `items`. Each item names its kind, short observation, measurements with native units, conditions, limitations, report/configuration/full-trace `source_refs` and any registered `clip_ids`. Retain source/CAD/config hashes, early ends and decisive failures. Upload selected videos through `tools/results_hub.py upload` to the existing private bucket. A kinematic preview, dynamic simulation, controller rollout and physical trial are different evidence kinds. Do not imply that a displayed pose proves motion clearance or achieved behavior.

Keep both dashboards readable: one short finding first, named measurement rows, and technical detail in labeled expanders. Do not paste reports into summary/observation text. Keep source evidence complete. Extend shared widgets for recurring displays instead of generating per-run HTML pages. See [the learning publishing guide](../../docs/operations/results-hub.md).

For a new run, keep the exact source/config/body/checkpoint hashes and outcome record with the native evidence. Publish one row with `workspace`, `observation`, `measurements`, `limitations`, and the complete `assets`/`clips` lists. Follow the [evaluation principles](../../docs/design/evaluation-principles.md) in every workspace: native-unit measurements, a written judgement, and per-dimension 0–5 distance-to-viable grades with confidence and trend in the row text, plus any requirement challenges for the user. No row carries a pass/fail verdict. The title and summary live in the table, so an `inspector.json` or per-run HTML page is unnecessary. Share `http://127.0.0.1:8805/?result=<result-id>` for learning or `http://127.0.0.1:8805/design/?result=<result-id>` for design. The Archive view finds older top-level MP4/WebM, GLB, PNG/JPEG/WebP, JSON/JSONL/CSV/Markdown and historical `index.html` files. A saved video, mesh, or report is not a fresh controller rollout or physical verification.

The historical skill dashboard's curated rows have been migrated to `data/results.sqlite`; it is no longer the publication source for new results. Keep historical policy verdicts in sealed transfer outcomes as provenance and measured cases in native matrices, then publish the concise observation, matrix and clip inventory as table rows. Cover every retained policy exactly once and label older-body clips as historical. A missing current video should be explicit, never filled by an unlabeled source-body replay.

For new low level training cycles, present each skill's **current candidate, best bounded saved version and challenger** separately. Lead with directly measured motion, duration, distance, contact, recovery and motor facts plus a plain-word account of how the robot behaved and where it degraded. Show matched smooth, rough, recovering and severe-failure clips. Do not calculate a composite or pass/fail training score; grade dimensions separately. Keep development and frozen historical gate counts linked as provenance, visually distinct from the new behavior review. Surface entry/goal envelope, teacher provenance, worlds, environment steps or PPO updates, GPU wall time and attributable cost. Label scripted teachers, imitation fits, reward-trained policies, camera-only probes and native contact results distinctly. Unknown or shared serverless charges remain unallocated rather than assigned to a skill.

For large parallel cohorts, use [the measurement aggregation contract](../../docs/research/ape-low-level-measurement-aggregation-20261016.md). Show native-unit command-error accuracy curves **with requested-horizon coverage**, plus p10/p50/p90/p99 distributions by skill and condition; a one-second fall must not appear accurate by omission. Show actual episode lifetime, terminal and infrastructure ends, unrecovered cases, event exposure and severe tails. Distinguish parallel world slots, complete episodes, independent seed families, transitions, updates and distinct checkpoints. Pair those curves with stratified source-matched clips and qualitative judgment; never compress unlike measurements into one score or turn each run into a binary verdict.

Verify that a published MP4 decodes, has the expected dimensions and visible frames, and is tied by seed, body, route and source hash to its native report and trace. For a full-duration video throughput check, decode and count the complete expected frame sequence, not only the first frame; record reset and termination counts separately from encoder timing. Say whether frames were captured during the scored visit or rendered later from retained state. Capture the worker's stdout, stderr and exit status for bootstrap, graphics attestation and native visits before stopping its pod. A graphics setup failure is an environment failure, not a failed physical-control episode; show its original log and cost when retained, or explicitly mark an unlogged terminal observation as such. Keep the recording missing until a valid one exists.

Show a prospectively frozen run that was stopped early as **operator-truncated**: display the original planned denominator, source-checked completed families, every attempted or failed original, and the decision time/reason. An incomplete planned cohort stays incomplete even if the completed subset looks favorable. A later imitation bank may use physically valid development episodes from such a run, but its learner validation must use new disjoint families.

For environment preparation, show the independent readiness review, its per-dimension grades and its evidence separately from policy results: exact body/interface, compiled contact endpoints, native reset/first action, curriculum exposure, teacher availability by skill, reward-versus-physical-outcome findings, measured worlds/GPU and open gaps. A successful setup probe is not a new skill version.

For seated-rise status, show V23 as the stopped 2,048-world CUDA reset failure with zero scored checkpoints and V24 as a distinct repaired-environment result: 418,952 accepted resets, zero recorded reset/capacity/nonfinite/first-step faults, fresh 6/6 scripted full rises versus 0/6 parked, and the [independent review](../../docs/research/ape-low-level-environment-v24-independent-review-20261014.md) that historically gave a GO for one capped diagnostic PPO pilot. Label the >15 mm sole-gap tail as unscored physically and learned recovery as zero. Keep older V16–V22 tests historical.

Show [V32b](../../docs/research/ape-cove50-seated-capacity-v32b-20261014.md) as the current same-closure seated **environment** capacity result: 2,048 CUDA worlds, 418,763 accepted entries, 383,947 partial resets, zero recorded capacity/first-step/nonfinite faults, the independent review's historical PASS (provenance) and stopped pod. It is the next setup check after V31d's one-world runtime sentinel. Keep V25 model25's 0/6 planted-stand learned score and the 6,230 unscored predicted >15 mm sole-gap entries visible as limitations. V32b contains no new policy checkpoint or skill score.

When an independent review finds a scorer or sensor fault, retain the original report, publish a versioned correction tied to the saved full-state source, and say whether the physical assessment changed. Source-match new success and failure videos to initial state, action and full physical trace. The seated recovery V5 example preserves its first report, corrects missed floor–pelvis contact by full-qpos replay, and still reports **4/6 scripted passes and two falls**; V6 verifies the corrected native sensor without calling it a new score.

For the current ape mechanical selection, read `robot/ape-current-design.json`. The inspector names and badges that artifact from the selection record; do not rename or edit sealed evidence to change the selected design. Record an intentional replacement in Idle and update the pointer.

Use the shared video, image, 3D, table and source-file widgets for new work. A standalone GLB should represent a complete scene or clearly named part; the 3D widget does not assemble a set of disconnected parts. Historical unique interactive pages remain embedded in Archive. Improve the shared widget when an interaction recurs. Details: [dashboard guide](../../docs/operations/results-hub.md).
